## tune-up-studio 0.4.0

Pieces that stop the scroll and lead to a message or a sale, for any business. The tune-up core stays 2.10.0.

- `referencias/` (new, English, examples in pt-BR from several kinds of business): `ganchos.md` (hook in three parts, opening families, objective and stage to families and call to action, scoring of 3 candidates, ad angles), `arquetipos-post.md` (post archetypes, the `l-*` arrangements with measures, type scale, carousel arcs), `padroes-cena.md` (12 CSS-only, seek-safe scene recipes, transitions, a persistent shape across cuts), `ritmo.md` (allowed scene patterns, arcs per objective, words per scene), `legenda.md` (caption structure, voice per tone, regulated niches) and `checklist-atencao.md` (12 items the session scores on its own snapshot, plus the closed list of AI tics). Each file under 150 lines.
