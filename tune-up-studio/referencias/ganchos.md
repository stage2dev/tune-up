# Hooks

Read by the post, video and anuncio skills in the Strategy step, before `estrategia.md`.
Every pt-BR line below is a mold from an invented business: copy the shape, never the fact. Each fact in a piece comes from the briefing or the brand kit.

## The hook has three parts
| part | job | post | reel scene 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| visual action | stops the thumb | the hero (heroi.md) or the photo, big | the hero wide in frame 0 |
| headline | opens the question | the `<h1>` | `s1_titulo` (+ `s1_destaque`) |
| support line | anchors the promise (often the offer) | one short line or a sticker | a device or `s1_selo` (up to 2 words); a longer line moves to scene 2 |

In reel scene 1, `titulo` + `destaque` + `apoio`/`selo` add up to **6 words at most** (QA `palavras_cena`). The three parts never say the same thing.
- Barbearia: chair icon + "Corte e barba antes do expediente" + sticker "sábado 8h".
- Oficina: photo of the worn brake pad + "Esse barulho tem 2 semanas de vida" + "revisão de freio com hora marcada".

## Speak to the reader, not about the offer
When the briefing names an audience or a situation ("quem trabalha sentado o dia todo", "quem busca o filho às 17h"), the headline talks to that person in that situation, and the offer moves to the support line or the CTA, named as the briefing names it (the service or package, not only its price). A question or a named pain is allowed, built only from the briefing's words and what its service implies; numbers only from the briefing.
- Pet shop, briefing "banho com busca em casa para quem trabalha o dia todo": "Sem tempo de&nbsp;levar o&nbsp;cão?", support "Banho com busca em casa". Never "Banho com busca em casa" as the headline: any pet shop posts it.
- Two sentences break one per line (`<br>` in the post; the second goes in `_destaque` in the reel), and every 1-2 letter word is glued to the next with `&nbsp;` (QA `legibilidade`).
- One of the 3 candidates is always a `reader's situation` hook when the briefing names an audience or a situation, whatever the objective.

## Pipeline
**Segment** (one person in one situation: "quem busca o filho às 17h e não tem onde deixar o carro", never "famílias") -> **motivation** (one pain, desire or objection, in the customer's words when the briefing has them) -> **family** from the menu -> the three parts. Write the 3 candidates from **3 different families** of the menu, at least 2 of them from the objective's row below. Three rewrites of one idea count as one.

## Opening menu (the only family names; write them exactly so in `estrategia.md`)
| family | mold (pt-BR) | needs | watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| reader's situation | `[Situação do leitor]?` / `[Situação]. E [a dor]?` | the audience or situation in the briefing | the offer goes to the support line |
| named pain | `Cansou de [dor]?` | the pain as customers say it | one pain, not a list |
| customer's question | `[Pergunta exata do cliente]?` | the question as typed | answer it in frame or scene 2 |
| objection | `[Dúvida]? [Resposta com fato].` | the doubt and its real answer | answer in the same piece |
| curiosity gap | `O detalhe que faz [resultado]` | the detail, paid off in the piece | never leave it unanswered |
| bold verifiable claim | `[Resultado] em [tempo]` | a fact the briefing states | proof in the next frame or scene |
| proof first | `[Número] [coisa] desde [ano]` | a real number | numbers only from the briefing |
| before/after | `[Estado ruim]. Agora [estado bom].` | two real states | no patient before/after in regulated niches |
| hyper-specific POV | `POV: [hora], [lugar], [problema]` | a precise moment | a vague POV is banned |
| choice | `[Opção A] ou [opção B]?` | two real options | the piece picks one |
| how it works | `[Resultado] em [N] passos` | a real process | show the steps |
| offer | `[Oferta] para quem [situação]` | a defined offer | condition and deadline only if written |
| number + deadline | `[N] [vagas/horários] para [dia]` | a real limit written in the briefing | no invented scarcity |
| real update | `Novo: [horário/serviço/endereço]` | a real change | say what changed and since when |
| list or signs | `[N] sinais de que [problema]` | the owner's know-how | deliver exactly N |
| myth vs truth | `Dizem: [mito]. Na real: [fato].` | verifiable knowledge | the fact settles it |

- Escola de idiomas: "Inglês para quem trava na hora de falar ao telefone" (offer). Pet shop: "Banho e tosa com busca em casa ou levar no colo?" (choice). Contador: "Seu MEI tem 3 prazos em maio. Você sabe quais?" (list or signs).

## Objective -> families
| objective | families to try first | default stage |
|---|---|---|
| vender | offer, proof first, before/after, objection | morno/quente |
| receber mensagens | customer's question, objection, reader's situation | morno |
| encher a agenda | reader's situation, number + deadline, hyper-specific POV, real update | quente |
| lançar novidade | real update, before/after, curiosity gap | frio/morno |
| ensinar e ganhar seguidores | list or signs, myth vs truth, how it works | frio |

`a IA escolhe`: infer from the **action** the briefing asks of the customer, never from a word that merely appears: reservar, agendar, marcar horário or consulta -> `encher a agenda`; comprar, pedir, encomendar, or a price or offer to act on -> `vender`; perguntar, pedir orçamento, chamar no WhatsApp -> `receber mensagens`; novo, nova, "a partir de" a date -> `lançar novidade` (with a buying action too: `vender`, the novelty in the hook or the support); dicas, sinais, "como" -> `ensinar e ganhar seguidores`; no clear action -> `receber mensagens`. Opening hours alone never set it. Write the result with ` (inferido)`.

## Short briefing (3 facts or fewer, no proof)
Hooks from `reader's situation`, `named pain` and `customer's question` (they need only the audience and the offer). Fewer frames or scenes, never one that describes the business (name, city, "atendimento individual"): the room goes to a bigger device. The offer is the payoff and the CTA, never the headline.

## Stage -> treatment -> CTA (`cta: <text> | leve|média|forte` in `estrategia.md`)
| stage | the reader needs | treatment | CTA | CTA molds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| frio | to recognize their own situation | situation, named pain, POV, curiosity | leve | "Salve para lembrar", "Siga para a parte 2" |
| morno | a believable way out | how it works, proof, before/after | média | "Mande OI e receba a tabela", "Veja os horários na bio" |
| quente | the objection answered | offer, guarantee, real limit | forte | "Peça pelo WhatsApp", "Garanta seu horário de sábado" |

A CTA names the action, what the person gets and where. Urgency only when it is true and written in the briefing; then the condition and deadline are on the art next to the CTA (QA `cta`): cut the support line before the deadline.

## Scoring the 3 candidates (one point each, 0 to 5)
1. passes the swap test: a competitor could not post the same headline (the offer alone fails it);
2. has a concrete: number, time, name, visible object, the reader's situation or the customer's words;
3. is true per the briefing (no fact added);
4. fits the limit: 40 characters in post and reel; in the ad, the `titulos` limit printed by `--plano`;
5. speaks to the segment in `publico`: names their situation or pain (an offer stated alone scores 0).

Tie: keep the reader's situation, then the one with a number. A label is not a hook: "Pão de fermentação natural" scores 0 on 1, 2 and 5. Barbearia: `[customer's question] Dá para cortar antes das 9h?` 5; `[proof first] 25 min, corte e barba` 4 (any barber claims it); `[rótulo] Barbearia tradicional do bairro` 0.

## Banned hooks
A generic POV ("POV: você merece"), "pare de rolar", "o que ninguém te conta" without the fact; a label (category or product name alone) or the bare offer when the briefing names who it is for; the logo or the business name first, in frame 0 or as the headline; a question answered by "sim" with no fact, or a headline that needs the caption to make sense.
