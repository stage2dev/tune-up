# Video style: produto em destaque

Read by the video skill only when this is the piece's style (`"estilo": "produto-em-destaque"` in `peca.json`); no other style file is read.
Distilled from the product-film prompts of the collection credited in NOTICE (one object carries the film, the camera frames a new side of it at each beat, the proof arrives as a change in the object). Rebuilt for the studio engine (one hero, no library, no network, no 3D, no sound), rendered and looked at frame by frame in round 7. The pt-BR lines are molds of an invented barber shop: never reuse one (QA `molde`).

## Use it when
The thing sold is an object the reader can picture (a dish, a garment, a car, a room, a screen) and the hero you can draw or the main photo is strong. It is the default when no other style fits.

## Slots of scene 2 (`rampa`, 7 words; 9 at 15 s)
| slot | limit | what goes there |
|---|---|---|
| `rampa.titulo` | 36 characters | the mechanism or the proof that continues the hook ("Corte e barba juntos"), never the business description |
| `rampa.apoio` | 40 characters | optional: how long, how many, for whom ("em 50 minutos") |

## Storyboard, 10 s
Cuts at 1.875, 4.375 and 6.875 s (this style keeps the long hook).
| time | scene | what the eye sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1.875 | 1 `gancho` | the hook at the top, its key words in the second color; a marker draws itself under them at 0.4 s; the hero wide on its stage, pushed in 13%, its problem parts hovering |
| 1.875-4.375 | 2 `rampa` | the hero in close-up, cut by the frame, on a light field; on the band below, the title; an accent line draws under it at 0.35 s; the support line rises at 0.55 s |
| 4.375-6.875 | 3 `oferta` | the offer's name on a tag, the price or offer word giant on the accent band, the proof; the hero changes: the problem parts fade, the `h_depois` parts pop one by one, the `h_traco` lines draw |
| 6.875-10 | 4 `final` | the call big, the condition on its tag, the contact beside the channel icon; a cursor travels to the contact and presses it at 1.3 s; logo and brand name; the hero 45% wide or more |

## 15 s
Cuts at 1.875, 4.875, 7.875 and 10.875 s. Scene 3 is `lista`: `lista.titulo` (30 characters) and 2 or 3 `lista.itens` (26 each), 9 words; the items are there at the cut and their ticks draw one by one. Then `oferta` (9 words) and `final` (12 words).

## Where the method enters
The hook is scene 1, whole in frame 0. The offer is scene 3: name + value + one fact not shown yet. The contact is the end card, as the briefing writes it. The hero's change is the payoff: draw 3 or more `h_depois` parts big enough to be seen from across the room.

## Never
- A second object: the film has one hero from the first frame to the last.
- A close-up title that describes the business ("atendimento de qualidade") instead of the mechanism.
- The price before scene 3.
