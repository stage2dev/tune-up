# Video style: mito ou verdade

Read by the video skill only when this is the piece's style (`"estilo": "mito-ou-verdade"` in `peca.json`); no other style file is read.
Distilled from the thesis-and-answer hits of the collection credited in NOTICE (a statement locked on screen, then a stamp that judges it on the beat, then the fact). Rebuilt for the studio engine (one hero, no library, no network, no 3D, no sound), rendered and looked at frame by frame in round 7. The pt-BR lines are molds of an invented frozen-food shop: never reuse one (QA `molde`).

## Use it when
The briefing answers a doubt or an objection people repeat about the product (it is expensive, it takes long, it is not for me) with a fact. Good for `ensinar e ganhar seguidores` and for the objection archetype.

## Slots of scene 2 (`mito`, 8 words with the stamp; 9 at 15 s)
| slot | limit | what goes there |
|---|---|---|
| `mito.frase` | 34 characters | the sentence people repeat, 3 or 4 words, no question mark ("Congelado é sem gosto") |
| `mito.selo` | `mito` or `verdade` | the verdict |
| `mito.resposta` | 34 characters | the briefing's fact that settles it, 3 or 4 words ("Pronto no mesmo dia") |

## Storyboard, 10 s
Cuts at 1.625, 4.375 and 6.875 s. Times inside scene 2 count from its cut.
| time | scene | what the eye sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1.625 | 1 `gancho` | the hook at the top, a marker drawing under its key words at 0.4 s; the hero wide, pushed in |
| 1.625-4.375 | 2 `mito` | the sentence big and whole at the cut; at 0.7 s the stamp (MITO or VERDADE, in an accent frame, tilted) slams in under it; the fact rises at 1.3 s and a bar draws under it at 1.6 s; the hero on its stage below |
| 4.375-6.875 | 3 `oferta` | the offer's name on a tag, the price or offer word giant on the accent band, the proof; the hero changes (`h_antes` fades, `h_depois` pops, `h_traco` draws) |
| 6.875-10 | 4 `final` | the call big, the condition on its tag, the contact beside the channel icon; a cursor travels to the contact and presses it at 1.3 s; logo and brand name; the hero 45% wide or more |

## 15 s
Cuts at 1.875, 4.875, 7.875 and 10.875 s. Scene 2 is the same, 3 s long. Then scene 3 is `rampa` (the hero in close-up over one more fact: `rampa.titulo` 36 characters + `rampa.apoio` 40, 9 words), then `oferta` (9 words) and `final` (12 words).

## Where the method enters
The hook of scene 1 is the reader's situation or the doubt as a question; scene 2 judges the sentence, so the two never use the same words. The fact is a number, a time or a way of working from the briefing. In a regulated niche the sentence is never about a competitor or a treatment's result. The contact on the end card.

## Never
- A sentence nobody says, invented to be knocked down.
- A fact that is an opinion ("É o melhor") or a promise.
- A sentence that names a competitor or another kind of business with scorn.
