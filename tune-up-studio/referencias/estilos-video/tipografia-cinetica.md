# Video style: tipografia cinética

Read by the video skill only when this is the piece's style (`"estilo": "tipografia-cinetica"` in `peca.json`); no other style file is read.
Distilled from the kinetic-type prompts of the collection credited in NOTICE (a locked list of short lines, one hit per beat, display type that fills the frame, one color for the last word). Rebuilt for the studio engine (one hero, no library, no network, no 3D, no sound), rendered and looked at frame by frame in round 7. The pt-BR lines are molds of an invented laundry: never reuse one (QA `molde`).

## Use it when
The message is a rhythm of 2 or 3 short phrases (a promise in steps, a contrast, a slogan the owner already uses) and the hero is plain. Good for `lançar novidade` and for services with nothing to show.

## Slots of scene 2 (`tipografia`, 8 words; 9 at 15 s)
| slot | limit | what goes there |
|---|---|---|
| `tipografia.linhas` | 2 or 3 lines, 14 characters each | the phrase cut where the voice pauses ("Lavou", "secou", "dobrou"); the last line is the gain and takes the second color; lines of 5 to 10 characters fill the width best |

## Storyboard, 10 s
Cuts at 1.625, 4.375 and 6.875 s. Times inside scene 2 count from its cut.
| time | scene | what the eye sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1.625 | 1 `gancho` | the hook at the top, a marker drawing under its key words at 0.4 s; the hero wide, pushed in |
| 1.625-4.375 | 2 `tipografia` | every line but the last whole at the cut, each as wide as the column, their words waving; the last line, in the second color, rises word by word at 0.7 s; a bar draws under it at 1.25 s; the lines pulse one after the other at 1.9 s; the hero stands small on its stage below |
| 4.375-6.875 | 3 `oferta` | the offer's name on a tag, the price or offer word giant on the accent band, the proof; the hero changes (`h_antes` fades, `h_depois` pops, `h_traco` draws) |
| 6.875-10 | 4 `final` | the call big, the condition on its tag, the contact beside the channel icon; a cursor travels to the contact and presses it at 1.3 s; logo and brand name; the hero 45% wide or more |

## 15 s
Cuts at 1.875, 4.875, 7.875 and 10.875 s. Scene 2 is the same, 3 s long. Then scene 3 is `rampa` (the hero in close-up over one more fact: `rampa.titulo` 36 characters + `rampa.apoio` 40, 9 words), then `oferta` (9 words) and `final` (12 words).

## Where the method enters
The hook stays in scene 1: the lines of scene 2 answer it, they never repeat it. The offer word of the briefing may be the last line when it is short ("grátis"), and then `oferta.valor` carries the price or the condition. The contact only on the end card.

## Never
- A sentence broken in the middle of a pair ("de / graça"): one line is one breath.
- Four lines, or a line of more than 14 characters: the type gets small and the style is lost.
- A line that is a mold of this file or a slogan the briefing does not have.
