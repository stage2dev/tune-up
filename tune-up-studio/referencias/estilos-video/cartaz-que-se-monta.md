# Video style: cartaz que se monta

Read by the video skill only when this is the piece's style (`"estilo": "cartaz-que-se-monta"` in `peca.json`); no other style file is read.
Distilled from the loud poster prompts of the collection credited in NOTICE (a street poster that moves: strips of flat color, type as the image, each piece landing on its beat, slightly out of square). Rebuilt for the studio engine (one hero, no library, no network, no 3D, no sound), rendered and looked at frame by frame in round 7. The pt-BR lines are molds of an invented second-hand bookshop: never reuse one (QA `molde`).

## Use it when
The piece announces something (an opening, a date, a sale, an event) and the words are stronger than any picture. Good for `lançar novidade` and `vender` with a short offer.

## Slots of scene 2 (`cartaz`, 8 words; 9 at 15 s)
| slot | limit | what goes there |
|---|---|---|
| `cartaz.blocos` | 2 or 3 strips, 14 characters each | the announcement cut in strips, 1 to 3 words each ("Feira de livros", "sábado", "na calçada"); each strip is one fact |

## Storyboard, 10 s
Cuts at 1.625, 4.375 and 6.875 s. Times inside scene 2 count from its cut.
| time | scene | what the eye sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1.625 | 1 `gancho` | the hook at the top, a marker drawing under its key words at 0.4 s; the hero wide, pushed in |
| 1.625-4.375 | 2 `cartaz` | strip 1 whole at the cut, a light band with dark type, tilted 3 degrees, with a hard shadow; strip 2, in the accent color, stamps in from above its size at 0.6 s on the other side; strip 3 at 1.2 s; the stamped strips pulse in a wave at 2.05 s; the hero on its stage below |
| 4.375-6.875 | 3 `oferta` | the offer's name on a tag, the price or offer word giant on the accent band, the proof; the hero changes (`h_antes` fades, `h_depois` pops, `h_traco` draws) |
| 6.875-10 | 4 `final` | the call big, the condition on its tag, the contact beside the channel icon; a cursor travels to the contact and presses it at 1.3 s; logo and brand name; the hero 45% wide or more |

## 15 s
Cuts at 1.875, 4.875, 7.875 and 10.875 s. Scene 2 is the same, 3 s long. Then scene 3 is `rampa` (the hero in close-up over one more fact: `rampa.titulo` 36 characters + `rampa.apoio` 40, 9 words), then `oferta` (9 words) and `final` (12 words).

## Where the method enters
The hook is scene 1; the strips are the announcement, never the hook again. A date or a place of the briefing may be a strip, as written. The price waits for scene 3, giant on its band. The contact on the end card, with the deadline in `final.condicao`.

## Never
- A strip with a sentence: 14 characters, 1 to 3 words.
- Strips that are adjectives ("Imperdível", "Incrível").
- More than 3 strips, or the brand name as a strip (it is on the end card).
