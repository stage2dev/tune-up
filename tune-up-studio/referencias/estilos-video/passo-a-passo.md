# Video style: passo a passo

Read by the video skill only when this is the piece's style (`"estilo": "passo-a-passo"` in `peca.json`); no other style file is read.
Distilled from the how-it-works beats of the collection credited in NOTICE (three steps on one line, each one arriving on its beat, the line drawing from one to the next). Rebuilt for the studio engine (one hero, no library, no network, no 3D, no sound), rendered and looked at frame by frame in round 7. The pt-BR lines are molds of an invented key cutting kiosk: never reuse one (QA `molde`).

## Use it when
The reader does not know how to buy or what happens after the message (a new service, an order with delivery, a booking). Three steps of 1 or 2 words remove the doubt. Good for `ensinar e ganhar seguidores` too.

## Slots of scene 2 (`passos`, 8 words; 9 at 15 s)
| slot | limit | what goes there |
|---|---|---|
| `passos.titulo` | 26 characters | 1 or 2 words ("Como funciona", "Em 3 passos") |
| `passos.itens` | 2 or 3 steps, 20 characters each | a verb and its object, in order ("Traga a chave", "Espere 5 minutos", "Leve a cópia"); 1 to 3 words each |

## Storyboard, 10 s
Cuts at 1.625, 4.375 and 6.875 s. Times inside scene 2 count from its cut.
| time | scene | what the eye sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1.625 | 1 `gancho` | the hook at the top, a marker drawing under its key words at 0.4 s; the hero wide, pushed in |
| 1.625-4.375 | 2 `passos` | the title, a vertical rail with its knots and step 1 whole at the cut; the accent line draws down the rail from 0.15 s; knot 2 fills and step 2 rises at 0.6 s; knot 3 and step 3 at 1.2 s; the last step pulses at 2 s; the hero on its stage below |
| 4.375-6.875 | 3 `oferta` | the offer's name on a tag, the price or offer word giant on the accent band, the proof; the hero changes (`h_antes` fades, `h_depois` pops, `h_traco` draws) |
| 6.875-10 | 4 `final` | the call big, the condition on its tag, the contact beside the channel icon; a cursor travels to the contact and presses it at 1.3 s; logo and brand name; the hero 45% wide or more |

## 15 s
Cuts at 1.875, 4.875, 7.875 and 10.875 s. Scene 2 is the same, 3 s long. Then scene 3 is `rampa` (the hero in close-up over one more fact: `rampa.titulo` 36 characters + `rampa.apoio` 40, 9 words), then `oferta` (9 words) and `final` (12 words).

## Where the method enters
The hook is scene 1 (the doubt or the situation). The last step is what the reader ends up with, not the call: the call is `final.titulo`. A step may carry a fact (a time, a place), never the price. The contact on the end card.

## Never
- A step the briefing does not describe, or a step that is a virtue ("Confie").
- Numbers typed before each step: the rail already orders them and every digit is a word in the count.
- Four steps.
