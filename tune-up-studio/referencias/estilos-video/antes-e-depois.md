# Video style: antes e depois

Read by the video skill only when this is the piece's style (`"estilo": "antes-e-depois"` in `peca.json`); no other style file is read.
Distilled from the before/after and two-phase prompts of the collection credited in NOTICE (the first state cramped and struck out, the second one wide and in the accent color, the object itself changing at the beat). Rebuilt for the studio engine (one hero, no library, no network, no 3D, no sound), rendered and looked at frame by frame in round 7. The pt-BR lines are molds of an invented upholstery cleaner: never reuse one (QA `molde`).

## Use it when
The offer changes a state the reader knows (dirty to clean, late to on time, three tools to one) and the briefing states both sides as facts. The hero you draw must have a clear `h_antes` and 3 or more `h_depois` parts: its change plays here, in scene 2.

## Slots of scene 2 (`antes_depois`, 8 words with the two tags; 9 at 15 s)
| slot | limit | what goes there |
|---|---|---|
| `antes_depois.antes` | 22 characters | the state before, 2 or 3 words, as the reader would say it ("Sofá manchado") |
| `antes_depois.depois` | 24 characters | the state after, 2 to 4 words, a fact of the briefing ("Seco em 4 horas") |

## Storyboard, 10 s
Cuts at 1.625, 4.375 and 6.875 s. Times inside scene 2 count from its cut.
| time | scene | what the eye sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1.625 | 1 `gancho` | the hook at the top, a marker drawing under its key words at 0.4 s; the hero wide in its problem state, pushed in |
| 1.625-4.375 | 2 `antes_depois` | the tag ANTES and its line in a dim color, the after line big under it, whole at the cut; a bar strikes the before line at 0.5 s; the tag DEPOIS stamps in at 0.95 s and the hero changes with it (the problem parts fade, the new parts pop); a bar draws under the after line at 1.1 s and the line pulses at 1.2 s and again, with its tag, at 1.9 s |
| 4.375-6.875 | 3 `oferta` | the offer's name on a tag, the price or offer word giant on the accent band, the proof; the hero stays in its after state, moving |
| 6.875-10 | 4 `final` | the call big, the condition on its tag, the contact beside the channel icon; a cursor travels to the contact and presses it at 1.3 s; logo and brand name; the hero 45% wide or more |

## 15 s
Cuts at 1.875, 4.875, 7.875 and 10.875 s. Scene 2 is the same, 3 s long. Then scene 3 is `rampa` (the hero in close-up over one more fact: `rampa.titulo` 36 characters + `rampa.apoio` 40, 9 words), then `oferta` (9 words) and `final` (12 words).

## Where the method enters
The hook is the before, said as the reader's situation; scene 2 pays it. The offer scene then lives on the name, the value and the proof. In a regulated niche (health, legal, finance) never use this style for a patient, a body or a result: checklist-atencao.md forbids that before/after. The contact on the end card.

## Never
- An after that is a promise ("Vida nova", "Pele perfeita"): only what the briefing states.
- The same noun on both sides ("Sofá sujo" / "Sofá limpo"): the after brings the new fact.
- A hero with no visible change: the scene has nothing to show.
