# Caption

Read by the post skill in the Copy step (`## Legenda` of `copy.md`); the video skill uses it for the suggested caption in `resultado.md`.
pt-BR lines are molds from invented businesses: swap every fact for one from the briefing.

## Structure
| part | rule | mold |
|---|---|---|
| line 1 | the reader's situation, pain or result, ≤ 125 characters (what shows before "mais"); never the business history or a greeting | "Seu carro chia quando freia? Isso tem prazo." |
| body | 2-4 short paragraphs (1-3 lines each) that complete the art, never repeat it | the art says "Revisão em 1 hora"; the caption says what is checked |
| "o que significa" | after each feature, what it means for the reader; the meaning comes from a fact in the briefing, never a comparison (dobro, mais que, melhor) nor a number that is not there | "Pastilha trocada na hora: você sai sem o chiado" |
| near the CTA | the main objection answered and the exact next step | "Não precisa marcar com antecedência: mande a placa e respondemos com o horário" |
| CTA | verb + what they get + where | `Mande "AGENDA" no WhatsApp e receba os horários de sábado` |
| ending | ends on the CTA or on a fact | "Atendemos até as 19h." |
| hashtags | 3-5 at the end: niche + product; a neighborhood or city only when the briefing or the brand writes it, never guessed | `#barbearia #cortemasculino #barbaemdia` |

Limits: whole caption ≤ 1000 characters; at most 5 hashtags. The QA checks the caption numbers like the art: each one (hashtags aside) must be in the briefing or the brand.

Line 1 molds: padaria "O pão de amanhã sai às 6h10 e acaba antes das 7h."; loja de roupas "Calça que não marca na cintura: 3 modelagens para provar."; contador "MEI tem 3 prazos em maio. O primeiro é dia 20."

## Forbidden
- Opening with "Olá", "Oi, pessoal" or the story of the company.
- Engagement bait: "Concorda?", "Marque um amigo", "Leia de novo".
- Emoji as a bullet or as structure; one line per sentence for the whole caption.
- A punchline ending ("Porque você merece."). End on the CTA or a fact.
- Any tic from `checklist-atencao.md`, or a word its regulated-niche list bans.
- Filler words that pretend to be facts (incrível, exclusivo, diferenciado, qualidade): replace with the fact they hide.

## Voice (`tom` in `marca.json`; none: `direto`)
`acolhedor`: "você", short sentences, one sentence about how the customer feels, no slang. `direto`: facts first, verbs at the start, a number where an adjective would go. `divertido`: one light joke at most, in line 1 or the CTA, never about the customer's pain. `formal`: "você" without slang, complete sentences, no abbreviations. `técnico`: the name of the method or material, then what it means in plain words.
